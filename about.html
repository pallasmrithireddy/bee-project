A living harvest

Every season leaves its own signature.

Our bee colonies are migrated seasonally to nectar-rich flowering crops. The flowers our bees visit influence each harvest's colour, aroma, flavour and texture.

Natural honey may crystallise over time. This is a normal characteristic of many genuine honeys and does not automatically mean the honey has spoiled.

Our beekeeping practices

  • Scientific beekeeping
  • Hygienic harvesting
  • Responsible colony management
  • Seasonal movement to flowering crops
  • Bee-friendly agricultural practices
  • Careful filtering without micro-filtration
