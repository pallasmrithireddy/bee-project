A NEW CHAPTER

When experience meets a passion for nature.

ATMA Beekeeper grew from a simple belief: honey should be handled with care, respect for nature and a commitment to honesty.

V. Kamalakar Reddy is a retired Prohibition & Excise Inspector with qualifications in B.Sc. and LL.B. Following his retirement, he dedicated himself to beekeeping and the work involved in producing and supplying natural honey.

His experience, curiosity and respect for the natural world continue to shape the values behind ATMA Beekeeper.

AT THE APIARY
HIS JOURNEY

From public service to the world of bees.

Retirement marked the beginning of a new chapter for Kamalakar Reddy. Instead of stepping away from meaningful work, he devoted his time to learning and practising beekeeping.

Beekeeping is a responsibility that extends beyond harvesting honey. It calls for attention to the health of bee colonies, the flowering plants around them and the environment on which they depend.

This understanding inspires ATMA Beekeeper's approach: respect the bees, handle every harvest carefully and remain committed to delivering honey with honesty.
