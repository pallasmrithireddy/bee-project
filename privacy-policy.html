Privacy Policy

How information is handled by this website.

Website demonstration: review this template and confirm the actual data practices before launch.

1. Information you provide

When you complete the checkout or contact form, you may enter your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and order details.

2. Information stored in this demo

This website uses browser local storage to remember cart items and a recent demo order on the device you use. These records are not automatically transmitted to an ATMA Beekeeper server.

3. WhatsApp orders

If you choose WhatsApp ordering, the website opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing the information needed for your order. Review the message before sending it. Once sent, WhatsApp and its applicable privacy terms govern that communication.

4. Payments

The current simulated payment flow does not process real payments and does not ask for card details. Do not enter financial credentials into a demonstration payment page.

5. Cookies and analytics

This template does not intentionally set analytics cookies. Additional services added in the future may collect data according to their own settings and privacy terms.

6. Data security and retention

Browser-stored demo information may remain on your device until browser data is cleared. This version does not provide a central customer account or server-based order history.

7. Your choices

You can choose not to submit a form or send a WhatsApp message. Avoid sharing information that is not necessary to your enquiry or order.

8. Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact atmabeekeepers@gmail.com or call +91 94400 13202.
